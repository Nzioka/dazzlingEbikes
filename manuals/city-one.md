# Dazzling E-Bikes City One Owner's Manual

## Specifications
Mid-drive motor 250 W, 65 Nm. Battery 500 Wh, range up to 80 km. Shimano Nexus 5-speed internal hub with belt drive. Hydraulic disc brakes, 180 mm front and 160 mm rear. Tyres 700x45c with puncture protection. Integrated front and rear lights. Maximum total load 130 kg.

## Tyre pressure and punctures
Inflate to 3.5 to 4.5 bar (50 to 65 psi), lower for wet roads. The tyres use Schrader (car-type) valves. To fix a rear puncture: shift to gear 1, unplug the hub gear cable at the clip on the chainstay, undo the two 15 mm axle nuts and lower the wheel out of the frame, then lift the belt off the sprocket. Refit in reverse, making sure the anti-rotation washers sit in the dropout slots, and tighten the axle nuts to 30 Nm.

## Belt drive
The carbon belt needs no lubrication. Do not oil it. Clean it with water and a soft brush. Belt tension is correct when the belt deflects about 10 mm under firm thumb pressure midway between the sprockets. If it squeaks, spray it lightly with water. A belt usually lasts 15,000 km or more. Replace it if teeth are missing or the belt is cracked.

## Gears
Shift gears with the twist shifter while pedalling lightly or stopped. If gears slip or jump, check the yellow marks on the hub are aligned in gear 3. Turn the barrel adjuster at the shifter to line them up.

## Brakes
The City One has hydraulic disc brakes. If the lever feels spongy or reaches the handlebar, the brakes need bleeding at a workshop. If the brakes squeal, clean the rotors with isopropyl alcohol. Replace the pads when less than 1 mm of pad material remains. Never get oil or chain lube on the rotors or pads.

## Lights
The lights run from the main battery and switch on with the display (hold - for 2 seconds). If one light does not work, check its connector behind the headtube or under the rear rack.
