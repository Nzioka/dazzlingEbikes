# Dazzling E-Bikes Fold & Go Owner's Manual

## Specifications
Rear hub motor 250 W, 40 Nm. Battery 360 Wh in the seat post, range up to 60 km. 7-speed derailleur. Mechanical disc brakes, 160 mm rotors. Tyres 20x1.95. Weight 19 kg. Maximum total load 110 kg. Folded size 80 x 40 x 70 cm.

## Folding and unfolding
To fold: put the pedals horizontal, fold the right pedal up, open the frame hinge lever and swing the front half alongside the rear wheel, open the stem hinge and fold the handlebar down, then lower the seat post fully. The magnets hold the bike folded. To unfold, reverse the steps and make sure both hinge levers close firmly and their safety catches click. Never ride with a hinge lever that does not close firmly.

## Hinge adjustment
If the frame or stem hinge feels loose or rattles, open the lever and turn the adjusting nut under the hinge a quarter turn clockwise, then close the lever. The lever should need firm hand pressure to close. Check the hinges every week if you fold the bike daily.

## Seat post battery
The battery is inside the seat post. To charge it on the bike, open the rubber cover under the saddle. To remove it, unlock with the key and pull the seat post out completely. Never use the seat post as a carrying handle when it is unlocked.

## Tyre pressure and punctures
Inflate to 3.5 to 4.5 bar (50 to 65 psi). Small wheels lose pressure faster, so check them every week. To remove the rear wheel, unplug the motor cable at the connector by the right chainstay, then undo the 15 mm axle nuts. Tighten the axle nuts to 30 Nm when refitting.

## Mechanical disc brakes
If the brake lever pulls too far, turn the barrel adjuster on the lever anticlockwise to take up cable slack. To adjust pad wear, turn the red dial on the inner side of the caliper clockwise one click at a time until the pad almost touches the rotor. Replace pads when less than 1 mm of material remains.
