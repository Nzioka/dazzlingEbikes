# Dazzling E-Bikes Trail Pro Owner's Manual

## Specifications
Mid-drive motor 250 W, 85 Nm. Battery 625 Wh, range up to 95 km. 12-speed derailleur. Full suspension: 140 mm air fork, 130 mm air shock. Hydraulic 4-piston disc brakes, 203 mm front and 180 mm rear. Tubeless-ready 29x2.6 tyres. Dropper seat post.

## Suspension setup
Set sag with the rider in full riding kit. Fork: 20 % sag, start at a pressure in psi equal to your body weight in kg plus 10. Shock: 30 % sag, start at a pressure in psi equal to your body weight in kg times 2.5. Use a shock pump only, never a tyre pump. Set rebound so the bike returns quickly without bouncing. Wipe the fork stanchions and shock shaft after every dusty or muddy ride.

## Tyres and tubeless
Recommended pressure 1.6 to 2.0 bar (23 to 29 psi) off-road, up to 2.4 bar on tarmac. The tyres come set up tubeless with sealant. Top up sealant with 60 ml per tyre every 3 months, more often in hot weather. Small punctures usually seal themselves: spin the wheel so sealant reaches the hole and re-inflate. For larger cuts, insert a tubeless plug, or fit a spare inner tube after removing the valve.

## Chain and gears
Clean and lube the chain after every muddy or dusty ride. If gears skip, check the derailleur hanger is not bent after a crash. A bent hanger causes poor shifting and can send the derailleur into the spokes. Replace a bent hanger rather than straightening it.

## Dropper seat post
Press the lever on the left of the handlebar and sit to lower the post. Release the lever to lock it. Press the lever without weight to raise it. If the post sinks on its own, it needs a workshop service.

## Brakes
Bed in new pads with 20 firm stops. Replace pads when less than 1 mm of material remains. Mud and dust wear pads quickly on red-dirt roads, check them every 2 weeks in the dry season.

## After a crash
Check the handlebar, stem and wheels are straight and tight, the brakes work, the derailleur hanger is straight and the battery is firmly locked. If the battery took a hard knock, follow the safety rules in the drive system manual.
