# Dazzling E-Bikes Cargo Max Owner's Manual

## Specifications
Rear hub motor 250 W, 85 Nm. Dual 500 Wh batteries (1000 Wh total), range up to 110 km. 9-speed derailleur. Hydraulic 4-piston disc brakes, 203 mm rotors front and rear. Tyres 20x2.4 front and 24x2.4 rear. Maximum total load 200 kg including rider, with up to 80 kg on the rear rack. Double kickstand.

## Loading and carrying children
Keep heavy loads low and centred over the rear wheel. Never exceed 80 kg on the rear rack. Children must ride in the approved Dazzling child seat or rear bench with footrests and the side guards fitted, and must wear a helmet. Always use the double kickstand when loading.

## Dual batteries
The bike draws from both batteries automatically. The display shows the combined charge. You can ride with only the main battery fitted, but range halves. Charge each battery separately or both on the bike using the charging port on the down tube. A full charge of both takes about 6 hours.

## Tyre pressure and punctures
Inflate to 2.5 to 3.5 bar (35 to 50 psi), use the higher pressure with heavy loads. Running low pressure with heavy loads causes pinch flats. The rear hub motor wheel has a power cable: before removing the rear wheel, unplug the motor cable at the connector on the left chainstay, aligning the arrows to separate it. Undo the thru-axle and remove the wheel. When refitting, tighten the thru-axle to 12 Nm and reconnect the motor cable, arrows aligned, until it clicks.

## Chain and gears
Clean and lube the chain every 500 km or after riding in rain. Use wet lube in the rainy season. If gears are noisy or skip, turn the barrel adjuster on the rear derailleur a quarter turn anticlockwise at a time until the shift is clean. Replace the chain when a chain checker shows 0.5 % wear. Cargo bikes wear chains faster than normal bikes.

## Brakes
The 4-piston brakes are designed for heavy loads. Bed in new pads with 20 firm stops from 25 km/h. If the brakes fade on long descents with a heavy load, stop and let them cool. Replace pads when less than 1 mm of material remains.

## Overheating on hills
On long steep climbs with a heavy load the motor may show E07 and reduce power. Shift to an easier gear so the motor turns faster, and use assist level 2 or 3 instead of Turbo.
