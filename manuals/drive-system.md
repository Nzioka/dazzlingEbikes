# Dazzling E-Bikes Drive System Manual (all models)

## Safety first
Switch the system off and remove the battery before any repair. Never open the battery, motor or controller: there are no user-serviceable parts inside, and opening them voids the warranty. If a battery is swollen, smells of burning, leaks or has been dropped hard, stop using it, store it outdoors away from anything flammable and contact the Dazzling E-Bikes service team.

## Battery and charging
Use only the Dazzling 4 A charger supplied with the bike. A full charge from empty takes about 4.5 hours (6 hours on the Cargo Max dual battery). Charge indoors between 10 °C and 30 °C. The charger LED is red while charging and green when full. For long life keep the battery between 20 % and 80 % for daily use and charge it to 100 % before long rides. For storage longer than a month, leave it at 50 to 60 % and top it up every 8 weeks.

## Removing and refitting the battery
Turn the system off. Insert the key in the battery lock, turn it anticlockwise and lift the battery out by its handle. To refit, slide the bottom contacts into the cradle first, press the top until it clicks, then turn the key clockwise and remove it. If the display stays dark after refitting, clean the contacts with a dry cloth and try again.

## Display and assist levels
Hold the power button for 2 seconds to switch on or off. Use + and - to choose assist level 0 (off), 1 Eco, 2 Tour, 3 Sport or 4 Turbo. Hold - for 2 seconds to switch the lights on or off. Hold + for 2 seconds to enter walk mode, which pushes the bike at up to 6 km/h.

## Error codes
- E01 Low battery voltage: charge the battery fully. If E01 returns right after a full charge, the battery needs a service check.
- E02 Battery over temperature: stop, switch off and let the battery cool in the shade for 30 minutes.
- E03 Communication error: switch off, remove the battery, check the display cable plug under the handlebar is pushed fully home, refit and switch on.
- E04 Throttle or walk-mode button stuck: release all buttons and restart. If it persists, the control pad needs replacing.
- E05 Brake sensor active: one brake lever is held or its sensor is misaligned. Release the levers. If E05 stays, check the small magnet on the lever sits within 2 mm of the sensor.
- E06 Speed sensor not detected: check the spoke magnet on the rear wheel is aligned with the sensor on the chainstay, with a gap of 3 to 10 mm. Tighten the magnet screw if it has slipped.
- E07 Motor overheated: the motor has reduced power to protect itself. Ride in a lower assist level or rest for 15 minutes. Common on long steep climbs with heavy loads.
- E08 Motor fault: switch off and on once. If E08 returns, do not ride under power: book a workshop service.
- E09 Controller fault: book a workshop service.

## Fixing a puncture (all models)
Tools: tyre levers, a pump, a spare tube or patch kit. 1. Switch off and remove the battery. 2. Remove the wheel as described in your bike's manual (on hub-motor bikes, unplug the motor cable first). 3. Let out any remaining air and push both tyre beads into the centre of the rim. 4. Lever one side of the tyre off the rim with two tyre levers and pull out the tube. 5. Run your fingers carefully around the inside of the tyre to find and remove the thorn, glass or wire that caused the puncture. 6. Patch the hole or fit a new tube with a little air in it, valve first. 7. Push the tyre bead back on by hand, starting at the valve, without pinching the tube. 8. Inflate to the pressure in your bike's manual, check the tyre sits evenly, refit the wheel and test the brakes before riding.

## Washing
Wash with a bucket of water, a soft brush and mild bike cleaner. Never use a pressure washer: water can be forced into the motor bearings and connectors. Remove the battery first and dry the contacts before refitting.

## Service intervals
- Every 2 weeks: check tyre pressure and brake function.
- Every 500 km or monthly: clean and lube the chain, check bolts are tight.
- Every 1500 km or 6 months: workshop service including brake pads, drivetrain wear, spoke tension and a firmware update.
- Every 2 years: brake fluid change on hydraulic brakes.

## Torque settings
Stem bolts 5 Nm. Handlebar clamp 5 Nm. Seat post clamp 6 Nm. Pedals 35 Nm. Brake caliper bolts 6 Nm. Disc rotor bolts 6 Nm. Thru-axle 12 Nm. Use a torque wrench: carbon and aluminium parts can crack if over-tightened.
